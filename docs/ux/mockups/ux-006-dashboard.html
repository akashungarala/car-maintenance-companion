The one detail that matters most

Every seeded item says assumed in words, next to the date. Until somebody marks an item done, we are guessing when it was last serviced.

A projected date presented as fact is a lie the product tells about the exact thing it exists to be trusted on — and the first time a user discovers a date was a guess is the last time they believe any of the others.

  • Empty bands are not rendered. An "Overdue · 0" heading is reassurance, and reassurance is not what a section heading is for.
  • "Overdue" is a word, not a red dot. Colour reinforces the heading; it never carries the meaning.
  • Dates are written out. 08/02/27 is two different days depending on where the reader lives.
  • "Not scheduled" is a real answer. A car that is not driven never needs its tires rotated, and inventing a date would be worse than saying so.
