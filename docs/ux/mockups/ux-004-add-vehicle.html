Decisions visible here

  • Three driving levels, not a number. Almost nobody knows their annual mileage, and a required number nobody knows is a wall. "Average" is preselected so the question can be skipped entirely.
  • No "when did you read the odometer". It is recorded as now. Dating it adds a field to justify a precision the input does not have — a few days of staleness moves a due date by hours.
  • Nickname last, and optional. Naming the car is the slowest part of the form and the least useful answer.
  • Values survive every failure. Losing five fields to a server hiccup is the fastest way to lose the user with them.
  • No VIN. It identifies the car but buys no maintenance schedule.
