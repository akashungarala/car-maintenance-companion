Decisions visible here

  • A 500 does not send you to sign in. Only a 401 does. Bouncing someone out because the server had a bad moment means they sign in, land here, and are bounced again.
  • Skeleton, not a full-page spinner. The page's structure is known before the answer is; replacing everything makes the screen flash on every visit.
  • No confirmation on sign-out. Its undo is signing in again. A dialogue here is friction pretending to be safety.
  • The empty state names what is missing. A blank area reads as a page that failed to load.
